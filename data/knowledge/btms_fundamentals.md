# Battery Thermal Management System (BTMS) Fundamentals

## Overview
A Battery Thermal Management System (BTMS) is critical for ensuring the safe, efficient, and long-lasting operation of lithium-ion batteries in electric vehicles (EVs). Batteries operate optimally within a narrow temperature range, typically between 15°C and 35°C.

## Purpose of BTMS
The primary purpose of a BTMS is to regulate the battery pack temperature. If the temperature is too low, the internal resistance increases, reducing power output and efficiency while increasing the risk of lithium plating during charging. If the temperature is too high, the rate of chemical degradation accelerates, reducing the battery's lifespan and increasing the risk of thermal runaway.

## Heat Generation
During operation (charging and discharging), batteries generate heat due to internal resistance (Joule heating) and exothermic electrochemical reactions. The amount of heat generated is directly proportional to the discharge rate (C-rate) and the square of the battery current.

## Operating Limits
- **Optimal Range:** 15°C to 35°C
- **Caution Range:** 35°C to 45°C
- **High Risk:** 45°C to 55°C
- **Critical Risk (Thermal Runaway Threshold):** > 60°C (depending on chemistry)

A well-designed BTMS must monitor the ambient temperature, coolant flow rate, and state of charge (SOC) to actively dissipate heat before the battery reaches critical temperatures.
